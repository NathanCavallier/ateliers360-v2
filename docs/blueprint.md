# **App Name**: Ateliers 360

## Core Features:

- STEM Education Modules: Offer workshops in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM) disciplines like physics, robotics, and AI.
- Interactive Workshops: Provide hands-on, engaging workshops for different age groups, promoting active learning and skills development.
- AI-Powered Education Tools: Offer introductory AI tools and modules including AI image/text generation for a new understanding of generative AI for use in society.
- Educational Catalogue and CMS: Create a detailed catalogue within the application showcasing modules with necessary fields such as a slug, complete description and goals. Should have CMS capabilites, the use of Supabase will be helpful.
- Contact form: Develop a targeted contact form where establishments that require conventions, and also should contain email-sending functions.
- AI Chatbot Integration (Future): Incorporate ImuChat for answering common questions. Develop the tool using pedagogical material to make the assistant informative.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary (Education): Deep Blue (#005B99) - Navy Blue: Confidence, Professionalism, Security, Stability. It's the primary color of the background and headers.
- Secondary (Innovation): Electric Cyan (#00A7C7) - Aqua/Tech Blue: Technology, Energy, Creativity, Future. Ideal for buttons (CTA), links and innovation icons (AI, Robotics).
- Tertiary (Fun/Vocation): Light Yellow (#FFD166) - Warm Yellow: Curiosity, Awakening, Light, Idea. Used in light accents to attract the eye or for 8-12 year old audience cards.
- Neutral: White, Light Gray (#F5F5F5), Pale Black (#222222): Page background, main text for maximum readability.
- Titles (H1, H2): Poppins or Inter: Sans Serif (Modern): Readable, round, but very professional. Evokes tech and simplicity.
- Body of Text: Roboto or Open Sans: Sans Serif (Neutral): Standard readability. Ensures reading comfort for module descriptions and educational objectives.
- Logo: Text Ateliers 360 + a simple pictogram (Ex: A light bulb + an atom/circuit). Simplicity and recognition. Use Deep Blue and Electric Cyan.
- Icons of the Pillars (6 Disciplines): Line or duotone icons (ex: Font Awesome Pro or simple Google Material icons). Avoid icons that are too complex or 3D. The flat or outline style is modern.
- Clarity and White Spaces. Use the light gray background (#F5F5F5) and lots of white space. An educational offer must be clear and easy to digest for the decision maker (teacher/director).
- Workshop photos real (children in action) or geometric/minimalist illustrations of the themes (circuits, planets). Avoid generic stock images. Show real kids smiling while coding/manipulating.
